Questions people ask, deadpan answers

Can I choose the lists?

No. That is the point. The schedule is drawn at random from all 520, so a sex megatoy warehouse and a haggis-hurling federation have exactly the same odds.

Will you email me?

Never. Not a welcome email, not a receipt (Gumroad sends that), not a newsletter, not a survey. If you ever receive an email claiming to be from us, it is not from us.

Can I get a refund?

No. The schedule is the service, and you have it the moment you pay. If you are in the EU or UK, you tick a box at checkout confirming you want the schedule generated immediately and waive the 14 day cooling-off period. If a list genuinely fails to sign you up, we swap in a replacement, which is the fix.

How do I make it stop?

Each list has its own unsubscribe link, and they all work, because we only pick lists that behave. If you want us to stop the remaining weeks entirely, email the address in the footer and we will halt your schedule.

What if a list shuts down?

Lists die. We check the whole pool every month, retire the dead ones and add replacements. If one of yours goes, your schedule page shows the substitute.

Is this legal?

You are subscribing yourself to public mailing lists. We just do the typing, on your instruction, with your consent recorded at checkout. The terms and privacy policy in the footer say the same thing at greater length.

Why does this exist?

We found it funny. Then we found 520 lists. Then it seemed a shame not to.
